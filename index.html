Recommended Bypasses

Bypass Wandegeya Congestion

Use Sir Apollo Kaggwa Rd → Makerere Hill Rd → Kasubi to bypass central Wandegeya bottlenecks.

Avoiding Clock Tower Jam

Take Ring Road → Mengo → Namirembe Road instead of going directly through Entebbe Road terminal.

Ntinda to City Center Alternative

Route via Bukoto-Ntinda Rd → Kisasi-Ntinda Rd → Kiira Road to save up to 20 minutes.
